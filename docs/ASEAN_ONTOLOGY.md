# ASEAN guide ontology repair

The ASEAN instruments expose several different object types that must not be
collapsed into one classification partition:

- seven guiding principles from the 2024 Guide;
- four organisational governance-framework areas from the 2024 Guide;
- six named GenAI risk objects carried into the 2025 Expanded Guide;
- nine policy-recommendation dimensions in the 2025 Expanded Guide.

The six GenAI risks are represented as multi-label flags with a power-set
codomain. The source names a risk set but does not state that each system must
belong to exactly one risk cell, that the risks are mutually exclusive, or that
they exhaust every possible harm. A system can therefore carry several risk
flags at the same time.

The frozen labels `Societal`, `Economic`, `Environmental`, `Security`, `Human
Rights`, and `Ethical` are not supported as the official six GenAI risks in the
cited ASEAN Guides. Proposition H7.2 must be retracted in its current
single-valued partition form or replaced with a typed relation comparing EU/VN
classification outputs to ASEAN principles, practices, policy dimensions, and
multi-label risk flags.

This ontology is source-parsed but still awaits immutable PDF hashes and a
second legal/policy review.
